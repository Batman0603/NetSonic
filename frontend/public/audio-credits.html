NetSonic audio credits

These sampled instrument sounds are loaded by the browser when an instrument is first selected.

  • Acoustic piano, flute, and nylon guitar: FluidR3_GM General MIDI SoundFont, created by Frank Wen and contributors; distributed in the MIDI.js soundfont collection under CC BY 3.0. Source and license information.
  • Wurlitzer keyboard: Wurlitzer EP200 Electric Piano by Greg Sullivan, mapped by kinwie; sample source and CC BY 3.0 license.
  • TR-808 drums: sampled TR-808 kit supplied through smplr and its sample collection.
  • Playback software: smplr, MIT License.
Instrument samples are separate from the NetSonic application code.
